About Me

I'm Joe Schlessinger, based in Washington, DC. This is where I write about what I'm working on and thinking about outside of work.

You'll find three kinds of things here: projects that use code and data to answer questions I couldn't stop wondering about, essays (often with a sense of humor), and book reviews along with a log of the books I've read since 2018.
